## 🎨 Forensic Sketch Artist Techniques Applied

### 1. **Facial Proportions** (Classic Rule of Thirds):
- Eyes: 1/3 down from top
- Nose: 2/3 down from top  
- Mouth: Bottom third
- Proper spacing maintained

### 2. **Feature Construction**:
- **Eyes**: Built from circles → shaped to lids → added iris/pupil
- **Nose**: Started with bridge → added tip → defined nostrils
- **Mouth**: Created upper lip M-shape → curved lower lip → joined

### 3. **Depth Creation**:
- Darker lines for foreground (eyelids, lip edges)
- Lighter lines for recessed areas
- Shading on side of face for contour
- Multiple stroke layers for texture

### 4. **Realism Enhancements**:
- Light reflections in eyes (makes them "alive")
- Cupid's bow on upper lip (signature detail)
- Nostril depth (not just outlines)
- Individual hair strokes (not solid shapes)
- Cheekbone definition (facial structure)
